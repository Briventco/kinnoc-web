## Not in this package, on purpose

- **Hero image.** The hero has no photograph. It is a mosaic built from the people photos, community monograms and three small UI snippets (see `components/asset-map.json` Hero). Do not add a hero photo.
- **Background images and textures.** Every section background is a flat token colour: white, mist (`#F4F6FA`) for the closing CTA and preview stages, ink (`#0E1B3D`) for Discover. No gradients, patterns or images.
- **Illustrations.** The design uses none beyond the phone status bar. Product previews are built in code, not images.
- **Social media icons.** The approved footer has no social links, so there are no social brand icons. Add them only when real accounts exist.
- **SVG logo.** No vector master was supplied. The PNGs are the official artwork; do not trace or redraw them. Ask for the SVG from whoever made the logo.
- **Red logo variants.** Exist in the brand files, excluded here.
