Website system and handoff

The site uses the same tokens as the app (Kinnoc Design System v2). This sheet adds what the web needs: type sizes per breakpoint, the grid, interaction states, motion, a component and section inventory, and the assets and placeholders still open.

Buttons are 56 (hero, CTA), 52 (sections), 44 (nav) and 40 (compact nav) tall, radius 12 (10 at 40). Active state nudges 1px down. Focus is a 2px ring 3px outside, blue on paper and #7FB8FF on ink. Every target is at least 44 × 44.

Motion

  • Hero entrance · label, headline, copy, CTAs rise 14px and fade in over 700ms, staggered 60 / 140 / 220 / 300ms.
  • Mosaic · tiles scale from 0.94 over 600ms, 40ms apart. Runs once on load.
  • Destination switch · preview and description fade up 6px over 280ms.
  • Hover · colour and background ease over 180ms. No lift, no scale, no parallax.
  • Typing dots · in conversation previews, 1.2s loop.
  • Reduced motion · all of the above off; the page is complete without them.

Assets needed before launch

  • Logo as SVG · lockup and symbol in blue, navy and white (only PNGs exist today), plus favicon set and a 1200 × 630 social image.
  • Five audience portraits · 4:3, at least 800 × 600, real people with signed consent, natural light, Nigerian settings.
  • Eight to ten face crops · square, at least 256 px, for the mosaic, previews and closing grid. Four supplied so far (Persons 1 to 4, 480 px crops), names pending.
  • Two community photos · square, for photo-style community avatars.
  • One event photo · 16:10, at least 1200 wide, for the Feed preview and event tile.
  • One resource cover · 3:4. Export all photos as AVIF/WebP through next/image.

The four supplied portraits are real people, cropped without retouching (see the mapping board). Every other photo is still a low-resolution placeholder cropped from earlier mockups.
